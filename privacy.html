PISB Carton Tracker – Privacy Policy

PISB Carton Tracker is an internal tool used by PISB Wedding & Home Deco staff, drivers and managers to register cartons and track transfers between PISB outlets and the warehouse.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address, only to confirm that you are a registered PISB user.
  • The work records you create: transfer and carton IDs, scan type (pickup, take over, receive), location, lorry number, staff ID and time.

The camera is used only to read carton barcodes on your device. No photos or video are stored or uploaded.

How it is used and stored

The data is stored in PISB's own Google Sheet and used only to run carton tracking, reports and issue alerts inside PISB. It is not sold or shared with anyone outside PISB.

Google user data

The app only reads your email address (scope: userinfo.email). Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
